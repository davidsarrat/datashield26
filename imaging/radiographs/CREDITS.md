# Chest radiograph credits

Six different real chest radiographs for the dsImaging album. Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and each file description page checked for author and the **CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication** on 2026-10-09. These are illustrative images, not a DataSHIELD patient dataset.

Licence: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

All assets are locally stored, at most 512 px on either edge, compressed JPEG (quality 85), with embedded metadata removed. No AI generation or retouching. Visual inspection of all six final images found no patient names, dates, record numbers, or other patient identifiers. Anatomical laterality labels (Dx/Sin) remain where present. `chest-02.jpg` is cropped by 210 px at the original top edge to remove its acquisition marker; the other five retain the full source frame.
